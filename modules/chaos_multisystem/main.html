One task, several chaotic systems

Each series is either a real chaotic series (label 1) or a fake with the same values and almost the same power spectrum (label 0). Only the order of the values, the determinism, can give the fake away.

Systems and sampling

  • Logistic map, Lorenz (x coordinate) and Rossler (x coordinate).
  • Each is recorded finely (λΔt = 0.5) and coarsely (λΔt = 2.0), where λΔt is how much a tiny error grows between two recorded points. Every row of the results is one system at one sampling.

Why several systems

A method that only learns the look of one system will fail on another. Comparing the systems shows whether a gap between dynamics-aware methods and shape-matching methods is general or belongs to one system.

Add your system

Add one entry to SYSTEMS in main.py (an ODE needs only its derivative, its largest Lyapunov exponent and a step size), then rerun it. Please check that your parameters are really chaotic.

Fakes are IAAFT surrogates, each the best spectral match of 10 attempts. Real and fake rows of a dataset come from different orbits. Series are z-normalised.
